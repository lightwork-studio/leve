Help with Leve

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email support@timweb.app. Leve is made by one person, so an answer can take a few days.

It helps to include the release shown at the bottom of Leve’s Settings, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you did before the problem appeared. Please don’t send real workout data unless I ask for it.

Sync and sign-in

  • Leve works without an account. Sign in with Apple in Settings to sync your workouts with your account.
  • Sync runs while Leve is open. Being offline is fine: changes wait on the phone and sync when you’re back online.
  • If Leve asks you a question about sync, such as which version of a changed workout to keep, it waits for your answer and never discards your changes silently. You can pause sync in Settings until you’re ready.

Deleting your data

  • To delete your account and everything synced to it, go to Settings in Leve and choose Delete account.
  • Workouts on your iPhone stay until you delete them or the app.
  • If you can’t open the app, email me with the email address shown for Leve under Sign in with Apple in your iPhone’s settings, and I’ll delete the account for you.

The privacy policy explains what Leve stores and where.

Reporting a problem

  • Email me, or open an issue in Leve’s public repository. Issues are public, so leave out personal details and workout data.
  • Report security vulnerabilities privately, as the security policy describes.
