A light app for heavy lifting.

Leve (Portuguese for “light”) is a workout tracker that stays out of your way. Last session’s numbers sit beside every set, the rest timer waits under your thumb, and pinned notes follow you to the next session.

01 Workout notes

Remember what worked.

Write down what you noticed during a workout and pin anything you want to see again next time. Leve keeps notes alongside the sets you logged.

  1. 01

    You jot it down

    Between sets, open the workout’s notes and type what you noticed. Pin a note to bring it back next time.

  2. 02

    Keep the context

    Find your notes alongside the workout where you wrote them.

  3. 03

    Pick up where you left off

    Review pinned notes at your next session and add a follow-up as you train.

02 History

Everything you lifted, kept.

Every session, set and record, sorted by month and charted lift by lift.

03 Switching Planned

Bring your Hevy history.

Hevy import is in the works. Once it lands, switching takes three steps.

  1. 01Export your workouts from Hevy as a CSV file.
  2. 02Import the file into Leve.
  3. 03Carry on with every past workout and set already in your history.
